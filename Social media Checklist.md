Responsible Social Media Use Checklist (For College Students)

PART B

**Do's**
 Think before you post – Ensure your content is respectful and appropriate
 Maintain privacy settings – Keep personal information visible only to trusted people
 Verify information before sharing – Avoid spreading fake news or misinformation
 Be professional – Remember that recruiters and professors may view your profile
 Use strong passwords & enable 2FA – Protect your accounts from hacking
 Respect others online – Follow digital etiquette and avoid offensive comments

 **Don'ts**
 Do not share personal details publicly (address, phone number, OTPs)
 Avoid posting inappropriate or offensive content
 Do not engage in cyberbullying or trolling
 Don’t accept friend requests from unknown people
 Avoid clicking suspicious links or ads
 Do not overshare your location in real-time
